AGE OF NUT - ORIGINAL LEG FRAMES

Original silhouettes, skin shading and nearby dark outlines retained. Brown/tan for Peanut; blue and light footwear for Hutch.
The original full-body source has weapon occlusion during death; those hidden leg pixels are not invented.
Fixed upper artwork is attached at the source tunic hem. Upper-body/arm motion is still the previous rig, not a tracing of the original arms.
Idle 50, walk 43, attack 40, die 23 frames, 30fps. Sound frame12, damage frame19 (zero-based).
Each baked full-body atlas is 8x8 with 320px cells and origin (160,270).
