CONTROLS

Watch the characters.

01
Choose your move

Click an enemy or use ← → to pick a target. Choose Jump, Shell Slam, your equipped special, or Silly Juice. Keys 1–4 work too.

02
Space as your attack hits

Jump: press as Peanut lands on the enemy. Shell Slam: press as the shells connect. Special moves: press as the projectile or shockwave hits. One well-timed press earns a second stomp or bonus damage. Watch the action itself.

03
Space as they hit you

Each rat winds up, then pounces, fires a bolt, or rolls toward you. Press right as it reaches Peanut to block the hit and take no damage. An early press uses your attempt, so wait for contact instead of mashing or holding the key.

Space, Enter, or a tap on the stage all work.
