HABIBI - AGE OF WAR MILITARY RIFLEMAN PUPPET, 1:1

Habibi drawn as the same 7-piece Flash puppet the original 2007 Age of War
military rifleman uses, posed by the original SWF timeline matrices frame for
frame. Average build. The rifle is replaced by his RPG.

Pieces (original SWF symbol): head (505), torso (511), upper arm x2 (501),
forearm with hand x2 (503), thigh x2 (507), lower leg with shoe x2 (509),
RPG launcher in place of the rifle (520).

Frames: idle 94, walk 47, attack 53, die 90, at 40 fps. Attack is the rifleman's
21-frame shoot clip followed by a 32-frame hold on its last frame, so a rocket
leaves every 1.33 s. The rocket fires on attack frame 10 (zero-based 9), from the
launcher tip at (+47,-65) game px. Baked at 3 px per Flash unit so he stands as
tall as Hutch. Atlases: 384x256 cells, 8 columns, ground point (192,222). In
Godot: frames.tres, AnimatedSprite2D scale 0.5, position (0,-47). This is what
the live Age of Nut player cave melee uses.
